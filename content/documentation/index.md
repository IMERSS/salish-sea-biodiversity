Our approach to detecting biodiversity change is documented in the
[Protocol for the Detection of Extirpation](https://imerss.github.io/detecting-local-extinction/docs/), which walks
through our framework for inferring local extinction using a tutorial dataset from Galiano Island. The protocol is
described in full in our article in
[*Plants, People, Planet*](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ppp3.70130).
