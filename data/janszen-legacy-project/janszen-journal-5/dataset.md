# Harvey Janszen’s field journal (1973–1980)

![The cover of Harvey Janszen’s field journal “Flora of Saturna” (1973–1980)](/images/janszen/journal-5.png)

Plant observations from the Southern Gulf Islands, transcribed from Harvey Janszen’s field journal of 1973–1980 through the Janszen Legacy Project. Records transcribed by Cindy Gao and Robin Bradley through the [Living Data Project](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/ldp.html) of the [Canadian Institute of Ecology and Evolution](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/) and available on [Canadensys](https://doi.org/10.5886/x4itxw). [Read the story of their work &rarr;](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/ldp-stories/vascular-plant-diversity-of-the-southern-gulf-islands-saanich-peninsula-and-san-juans)
