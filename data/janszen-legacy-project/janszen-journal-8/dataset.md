# Harvey Janszen’s field journal (1997–1999)

![The cover of Harvey Janszen’s field journal (1997–1999)](/images/janszen/journal-8.png)

Plant observations from Texada Island, transcribed from Harvey Janszen’s field journal of 1997–1999 through the Janszen Legacy Project. Records transcribed through the [Living Data Project](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/ldp.html) of the [Canadian Institute of Ecology and Evolution](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/) and available on [Canadensys](https://doi.org/10.5886/x4itxw).
