# *Woodwardia fimbriata* on Texada Island (1997)

Harvey Janszen’s 1997 survey assessing the status of the giant chain fern (*Woodwardia fimbriata*) on Texada Island. Records note the size and condition of each population, including sites since destroyed.

GPS coordinates predate May 2000, when the US military still degraded civilian GPS signals, and may be off by up to ~100 m.
