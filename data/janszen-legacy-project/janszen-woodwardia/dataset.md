# *Woodwardia fimbriata* on Texada Island (1997)

Harvey Janszen’s 1997 survey assessing the status of the giant chain fern (*Woodwardia fimbriata*) on Texada Island. Records note the size and condition of each population, including sites since destroyed. Records transcribed through the [Living Data Project](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/ldp.html) of the [Canadian Institute of Ecology and Evolution](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/) and available on [Canadensys](https://doi.org/10.5886/x4itxw). Read the stories behind this work: [2023](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/ldp-stories/harvey-janszen-legacy-project), [2024](https://www.ciee-icee.ca/ldp-stories/harvey-janszen-legacy-project-part-2).

GPS coordinates predate May 2000, when the US military still degraded civilian GPS signals, and may be off by up to ~100 m.
