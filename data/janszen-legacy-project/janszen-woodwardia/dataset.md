# *Woodwardia fimbriata* on Texada Island (1997)

Harvey Janszen’s 1997 survey of giant chain fern (*Woodwardia fimbriata*) on Texada Island, assessing the status of the
species there. Records note the size and condition of each population, including sites since destroyed.

GPS coordinates predate May 2000, when the US military still degraded civilian GPS signals, and may be off by up to ~100 m.
