# Diatoms of the Salish Sea (1866–2026)

A catalogue of marine diatom occurrences across the Salish Sea, consolidating records from the published literature,
morphological study of samples and molecular (DNA metabarcoding) surveys. Together, these records document the
region's diatom flora and how knowledge of it has accumulated since the first reports in 1866.

Data paper: [Webber *et al.* (2026), *Biodiversity Data Journal* 14: e189060](https://doi.org/10.3897/BDJ.14.e189060)
