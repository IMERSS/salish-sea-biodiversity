# Marine animals of Galiano Island (1893–2021)

A catalogue of the marine animals reported from the waters around Galiano Island, from sponges and worms to fishes
and marine mammals. It brings together historical collections and published reports with the community science
observations gathered by the Biodiversity Galiano project since 2016, distinguishing historical reports that have
since been confirmed, those that remain unconfirmed, and species newly reported for the island.
