        Documentation

        Our approach to detecting biodiversity change is documented in the Protocol for the Detection of Extirpation, which walks through our framework for inferring local extinction using a tutorial dataset from Galiano Island. The protocol is described in full in our article in Plants, People, Planet. Aspects of this protocol have been adapted in the bioReportR framework, with examples presented here for the Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound Biosphere and Saanich Peninsula.

        Xetthecum: a learning journey follows the Xetthecum ecocultural mapping pilot at Retreat Cove, Galiano Island, through its three phases, from November 2020 to April 2024.
