      Projects

      Frameworks for each of our studies. Each project is also outlined on the map - click its boundary to visit it.

      Flora of the Saanich Peninsula

      An analysis of the vascular plant flora of the Saanich Peninsula, drawing on historical collections and community science observations to map the status of the region’s plants, track species that have not been reported in recent decades, and recognise the contributors whose records make this work possible.

      Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound Biosphere Vegetation Report

      A map-based report on the vascular plant diversity of the Átl’ḵa7tsem/Howe Sound Biosphere Region, relating historical and community science records to biogeoclimatic zones and protected areas across the biosphere.
