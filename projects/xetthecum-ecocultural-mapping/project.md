# *Xetthecum* Ecocultural Mapping Pilot

An initiative weaving together Indigenous ways of knowing and Western ecological science into an ecocultural map of
Retreat Cove (*Xetthecum* in the Hul’qumi’num language), Galiano Island. Created in partnership with Indigenous and
non-Indigenous collaborators, this participatory mapping program brings together historical and contemporary
biodiversity data, terrestrial ecosystem mapping, written and spoken Hul’qumi’num, and stories of place, fostering a
holistic approach to shle’muxun—“guardian of the land,” or “stewardship,” in Hul’qumi’num.

Documentation: [*Xetthecum*: a learning journey](https://imerss.github.io/xetthecum-documentation/docs/)
