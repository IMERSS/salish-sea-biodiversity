# *Xetthecum* Ecocultural Mapping Pilot

A pilot project, carried out by IMERSS with Indigenous and non-Indigenous collaborators through the Spirit of the
Sxwo’le Coalition, to create an ecocultural map of *Xetthecum* (Retreat Cove), Galiano Island. Running from 2020 to
2024, the pilot wove together Indigenous ways of knowing and Western ecological science, drawing on historical and
contemporary biodiversity data, terrestrial ecosystem mapping, written and spoken Hul’q’umi’num’, and stories of
place—in support of a holistic approach to shle’muxun—“guardian of the land,” or “stewardship.”

Documentation: [*Xetthecum*: a learning journey](https://imerss.github.io/xetthecum-documentation/docs/)
